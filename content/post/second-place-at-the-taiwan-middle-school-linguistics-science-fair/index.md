I'm excited to share that the students I mentored won **second place** at the **Taiwan Middle School Linguistics Science Fair**!

Their project, *"Was That Sarcasm? Deciphering Intonation and Syntax in Adolescent Sarcasm Detection,"* explored how adolescents interpret intonational and syntactic cues to recognize sarcasm.

It was a pleasure to guide them through the research process and see their hard work and curiosity recognized. Congratulations to the team on this wonderful achievement!
